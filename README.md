# Business Contact Organization

## Project Overview

This project simulates a real-world freelance task involving
the organization and cleaning of business contact data.

The dataset contains 130 fictional business contacts.
The objective was to standardize the records, identify
data quality issues, and prepare a client-ready Excel file.

## Client Requirements

The client requested the following tasks:

- Organize and standardize business contact records.
- Identify missing and invalid information.
- Detect duplicate Contact IDs.
- Preserve the original data.
- Document unresolved issues for client review.
- Deliver a clean contact list and a summary report.

## Work Completed

- Organized 130 fictional business contact records.
- Removed unnecessary spaces from contact names.
- Corrected 6 malformed email addresses.
- Identified 3 duplicate Contact ID issues.
- Identified 5 missing phone numbers.
- Identified 4 missing contact statuses.
- Created an Issue Log for unresolved problems.
- Prepared a clean contact list and a summary report.

## Quality Assurance

The following quality checks were performed:

- Verified the total number of contact records.
- Compared cleaned names with the working dataset.
- Validated the transfer of corrected email addresses.
- Checked data consistency across the remaining columns.
- Used COUNTIF to identify and summarize data issues.
- Confirmed that the issue counts matched the summary report.
- Verified the final Excel file before delivery.
